What is Futbot?

Futbot is a football companion for players, parents and coaches. Pick your team and get its season in one place, then ask the Futbot assistant anything about it in plain language.

What can it do?

  • Matches and matchdays: results, fixtures and match details.
  • Standings: the league table for your team's competition.
  • Squad and players: browse the squad and individual player pages.
  • Top scorers: the Pichichi ranking.
  • Chat with Futbot: ask questions about your selected team and get answers in English, Spanish or Catalan.
  • Team tracking: follow multiple teams and switch between them.
  • Player tracking: save players to your list and see their stats and next match.
  • Android notifications: manage optional final-result alerts for teams you follow.
  • AI match forecasts: explore the next match using standings, home and away form, and recent results.
  • AI 1X2 quiniela: see the current matchday’s favorites and the reasons behind each pick. X means no clear favorite; matches without enough data have no pick.
  • Club information: discover your club, its teams and available official links.

How to install

Android

  3. If Android asks, allow installing apps from this source.
